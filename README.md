Understanding Artificial Intelligence: The Basics

Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, and self-correction. Courses designed to teach AI typically cover a broad spectrum of topics, including machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and neural networks. The complexity and depth of these subjects can be daunting for beginners, but a structured course can demystify these concepts and provide practical insights.
